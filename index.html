About Us

ATOM GYM AND FITNESS (UNISEX)

Atom Gym and Fitness is Puducherry's most trusted unisex gym, nestled in the heart of Neravy. With an exceptional 4.9-star rating and a community of dedicated members, we offer a clean, welcoming, and fully-equipped fitness environment for everyone — beginners, enthusiasts, and advanced athletes alike. Our state-of-the-art facilities and diverse fitness classes make every workout session engaging, effective, and enjoyable.

At Atom Gym, we believe fitness is a journey, not a destination. Our highly trained and friendly coaches — including expert trainers Rajasekaran and Ezhil — deliver personalized training programs tailored to your unique goals. Whether you want to lose weight, build strength, or simply live a healthier lifestyle, Atom Gym is your partner every step of the way. Open six days a week with extended hours, we make it easy for you to prioritize your health on your schedule.
